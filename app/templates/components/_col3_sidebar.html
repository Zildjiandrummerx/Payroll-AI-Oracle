Legal Guide

AFP (7.25%): Your contribution to your pension fund. Your employer also contributes an additional 8.75% on your behalf.

ISSS (3%): Social Security deduction, capped at a maximum of $30.00 monthly. Employer pays an additional 7.5%.

Employees working on a mandatory holiday earn double their standard salary for that day.

  • January 1st (New Year)
  • Holy Thursday, Friday, and Saturday (Easter)
  • May 1st (Labor Day)
  • May 10th (Mother's Day)
  • June 17th (Father's Day)
  • August 6th (El Salvador del Mundo)
  • September 15th (Independence Day)
  • November 2nd (All Souls' Day)
  • December 25th (Christmas)

14th Month: Paid Jan 15-25. Equivalent to 50% of your monthly salary. Exempt from ISR, ISSS, and AFP.

Vacation Bonus: Corresponds to 30% of a 15-day salary. Not subject to statutory deductions.

Aguinaldo (Christmas Bonus): Must be paid by Dec 20th. Based on years of service:

  • 1 to 3 years: Equivalent to 15 days salary.
  • 3 to 10 years: Equivalent to 19 days salary.
  • 10+ years: Equivalent to 21 days salary.
